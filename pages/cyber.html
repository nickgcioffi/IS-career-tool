"Cyber security is about protecting people, systems, and information in an increasingly connected world."

Day-to-Day Work

Cyber security professionals monitor systems, investigate suspicious activity, respond to security incidents, identify vulnerabilities, and help organizations protect their data.

Technical Skills & Tools

Common skills include networking, Linux, Windows administration, scripting, cloud security, vulnerability assessment, and security monitoring. Tools may include SIEM platforms, Wireshark, Nmap, and vulnerability scanners.

Entry-Level Expectations

Interns and entry-level candidates should understand basic networking, operating systems, security concepts, and common cyber threats. Hands-on labs, projects, coursework, and certifications can help demonstrate practical knowledge.

Salary & Career Growth

Entry-level cyber security positions can provide a strong starting salary, with opportunities to advance into specialized security, engineering, consulting, and leadership positions as experience grows.

What Makes a Strong Candidate?

Strong candidates combine technical ability with curiosity, problem-solving skills, attention to detail, communication, and a willingness to keep learning. Employers value candidates who can demonstrate what they know through projects, labs, internships, certifications, or other hands-on experience.
