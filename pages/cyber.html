Cyber Security

Cybersecurity professionals protect organizations, systems, networks, and information from cyber threats. This field offers a variety of career opportunities ranging from entry-level security analysts to executive leadership positions.

Protect. Detect. Respond.

Cybersecurity professionals help protect digital systems, information, and organizations.

"Cybersecurity is not just about technology. It is about protecting people, information, and organizations."

Cybersecurity Career Overview

Career Responsibilities

What Does a Cybersecurity Professional Do?

A typical day involves protecting systems, investigating threats, and preventing attacks.

Monitor & Investigate

Professionals monitor systems for suspicious activity, review security alerts, analyze logs, and investigate possible security incidents.

Protect Systems

Cybersecurity teams identify vulnerabilities, strengthen security controls, protect networks, and help prevent unauthorized access.

Respond to Threats

When an incident occurs, cybersecurity professionals help contain the threat, investigate what happened, and improve defenses against future attacks.

Technical Knowledge

Skills & Tools

Cybersecurity requires technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, and attention to detail.

Important Technical Skills

  • Networking fundamentals
  • Windows and Linux operating systems
  • Security monitoring and threat detection
  • Basic scripting and programming
  • Vulnerability management
  • Incident response
  • Cloud security fundamentals
  • Cybersecurity frameworks

Common Security Tools

Cybersecurity professionals may work with SIEM platforms, vulnerability scanners, endpoint security tools, network analysis tools, and command-line utilities.

Getting Started

Entry-Level & Internship Expectations

You do not need to know everything before starting. A strong foundation and willingness to learn matter.

Start Building Experience

Entry-level and internship candidates are not expected to know everything. Employers typically look for a strong foundation and a willingness to learn.

  • Basic networking knowledge
  • Understanding of computer systems
  • Knowledge of common cybersecurity threats
  • Cybersecurity coursework or training
  • Personal projects or cybersecurity labs
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills
  • Willingness to continue learning
Career Growth

Salary & Growth Trajectory

Cybersecurity careers can provide opportunities for increasing responsibility and compensation.

Entry-Level

$60,000–$80,000

Per year for junior roles like SOC analysts or support positions.

Mid-Level

$90,000–$120,000

Per year with approximately 3 to 5 years of experience.

Senior / Management

$120,000–$170,000+

Security engineers, managers, and security architects.

Executive (CISO)

$175,000–$400,000+

Chief Information Security Officers, depending on company size.
