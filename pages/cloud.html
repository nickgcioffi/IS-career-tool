    A practical BYU IS roadmap

    Turn classes into proof.

    A certification can help your résumé get found. A small, documented system proves you can do the work. Aim to leave each semester with both vocabulary and evidence.

    01

    Learn the system

    Understand networking, Linux, identity, databases, and how a web request moves through a cloud architecture.

    02

    Build one real deployment

    Deploy an app with infrastructure as code, least-privilege access, monitoring, a budget alert, and a clear README.

    03

    Practice the story

    Explain the business need, your tradeoffs, the failure you diagnosed, the cost, and what you would improve next.

    04

    Target adjacent internships

    Search beyond “cloud intern”: systems, platform, IT operations, solutions, security, DevOps, and technical consulting roles all build relevant experience.
