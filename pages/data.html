INFORMATION SYSTEMS CAREER

Data Analytics

Turn data into insights that help businesses make better decisions. Data analysts combine technology, statistics, and business knowledge to understand what the numbers are saying.

Explore the Role

DATA

Turn information into insights.

Data analytics connects business questions with data-driven answers.

Collect

Gather information from databases, spreadsheets, business systems, and other data sources.

Analyze

Clean data, identify patterns, calculate metrics, and investigate important business questions.

Visualize

Build dashboards, charts, and reports that make information easier to understand.

Explain

Present findings and explain what the data means for the business.

INSIGHTS

Skills & Tools

Start with the fundamentals. As you gain experience, you can add more advanced tools and techniques.

SQL

Query databases and work with structured data.

Excel

Organize, clean, analyze, and visualize information.

Power BI / Tableau

Create dashboards and communicate data visually.

Python

Automate analysis and work with larger datasets.

Ready for an Internship?

You don't need to know everything. Focus on building a strong foundation and showing that you can apply what you know.

Basic SQL

SELECT, WHERE, JOIN, GROUP BY, and aggregate functions.

Excel

Formulas, filtering, pivot tables, charts, and data cleaning.

Basic Statistics

Understand averages, distributions, trends, and relationships.

Dashboard Project

Create a dashboard that shows useful business insights.

Portfolio

Have one or two projects that demonstrate your skills.

Communication

Explain what your analysis means to non-technical audiences.

Salary & Career Growth

Compensation varies based on experience, location, industry, and specialization.

Entry Level

$55K–$75K

A typical planning range for interns, recent graduates, and junior analysts.

Experienced

$75K–$100K+

Analysts with stronger technical skills and several years of experience.

Senior / Specialized

$100K+

Senior analysts, analytics leaders, and specialized professionals.

These are approximate U.S. planning ranges, not guaranteed salaries.

Where Can This Career Go?

Data Analytics can lead to several different technology and business careers.

Data Analyst

Start by analyzing data, building reports, creating dashboards, and answering business questions.

Senior Data Analyst

Take on more complex analysis, work with stakeholders, and influence business decisions.

Analytics Lead / Manager

Lead analytics projects, mentor analysts, and help organizations make data-driven decisions.

Other directions

Business Intelligence  •  Product Analytics  •  Analytics Engineering  •  Data Science

What Makes a Strong Candidate?

The strongest candidates combine technical skills with business understanding and communication.

Analytical

You enjoy asking questions, finding patterns, solving problems, and understanding why something is happening.

Technical

You can work with data using tools such as SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, or Tableau.

Communicative

You can explain your findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.

Why IS?

Data Analytics sits at the intersection of business and technology. Information Systems students can use their understanding of both areas to turn data into meaningful business decisions.
